Products

What we build

Standard platforms and custom-engineered units, built around fully digital control.

UPS Systems

Single and three-phase online UPS with low-THD output and high overload capability.

  • Active front end (low input harmonics)
  • N+1 parallel redundant inverters
  • Thyristor and IGBT-based options

Railway Converters

Converters and controllers for rolling stock and railway workshops, designed for demanding environments.

  • Auxiliary converters
  • Regenerative battery chargers (RBC)
  • Control and monitoring electronics

Battery Chargers

Constant-current / constant-voltage chargers for lead-acid, NiCd and Li-ion banks in utility and industrial duty.

  • Thyristor and high-frequency IGBT/SiC types
  • N+1 modular configurations
  • Charge and discharge capability

EV Chargers

Power stages and control for AC and DC electric-vehicle charging, with grid-friendly input.

  • Isolated, high-efficiency conversion
  • Smart control and monitoring

High-end DC-DC Converters

Phase-shifted full bridge, dual active bridge and resonant topologies for high power and high current.

  • Wide input and output ranges
  • Bidirectional options
  • Custom magnetics

Controllers and Monitoring

Control boards and firmware for thyristor rectifiers, ESP power supplies and battery systems, with Modbus communication.

  • ESP spark-control boards
  • Battery and earth-leakage monitors
