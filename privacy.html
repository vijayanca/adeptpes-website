Privacy policy

Adept Power Electronics Solutions ("we") runs adeptpes.in. This page explains what information the website collects and how we use it.

Enquiries you send us

When you submit the enquiry form, we receive the name, company, email, phone number and requirement details you enter. The form is delivered to our email through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal. We do not sell or share them with anyone else.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising, such as how many visitors send an enquiry or tap to call. These services use cookies and similar technologies and may collect information such as your device, browser, approximate location and the pages you visit. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and manage ad personalisation in your Google ad settings.

WhatsApp, phone and email

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, those conversations are handled by the respective service and used only to respond to you.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email sales@adeptpes.in.
